What buyers like

  • Reviewers praise deep blacks, rich colour, HDR and wide viewing angles.

    Good to know: It looks its best in a darker room. The 2 complaints are about sunlight washing it out and faint banding (R3, R13).

    “Blacks are truly black and HDR movies look incredible in a dark room.” R1, SampleMart

    “Colours and contrast are excellent.” R2, DemoShop

  • Smooth 144Hz with VRR and low input lag on PS5 and Xbox.

    Good to know: Only two HDMI ports support full 144Hz, so plug consoles into those (R16).

    “Gaming on PS5 is fantastic.” R4, SampleMart

    “The game mode is superb.” R27, ShopLane

  • Simple to use, responds instantly and charges from room light.

    Good to know: Turning off HDMI-CEC to stop soundbar dropouts means the remote can no longer control volume (R18).

    “The remote is comfortable and responds instantly.” R36, SampleMart

    “Picture is gorgeous and the remote charges from room light, which is a nice touch.” R8, DemoShop

  • Seen as good value on sale. One reviewer felt the launch price was high.

    Good to know: Both reviewers bought it on sale (R22, R30).

    “Great value for a 65-inch OLED on sale.” R30, SampleMart

    “Worth it at the sale price.” R22, SampleMart

  • Setup took about ten minutes, with a simple remote.

    “Setup took ten minutes and the remote is simple to use.” R25, SampleMart

Recurring concerns

  • Sponsored rows on the home screen (2 reviews) and problems that started after firmware updates (4 reviews).

    What reviewers did: No reviewer found a way to turn the ads off. One says the September update fixed their update-related problem (R32).

    How to avoid it: Install the latest firmware when you set it up.

    “The home screen is full of ads and sponsored rows, which is annoying on a TV this expensive.” R5, DemoShop

    “The home screen ads are intrusive and the menus feel slow.” R29, DemoShop

  • Soundbar audio cuts out every few minutes or several times an evening.

    When it shows up: After the June 2026 update.

    What reviewers did: The September update fixed it for one reviewer (R32). Turning off HDMI-CEC stops the dropouts, but the TV remote then can’t control volume (R18). A factory reset didn’t help (R19).

    How to avoid it: Update the TV before connecting a soundbar.

    “After the latest update the eARC audio cuts out randomly every few minutes.” R19, ShopLane

    “The eARC audio still drops out with my soundbar even after the August update.” R31, ShopLane

  • Thin speakers, weak bass and dialogue that’s hard to hear.

    What reviewers did: 3 of the 4 reviewers mention a soundbar, and one says it fixed the problem (R35).

    How to avoid it: Plan for a soundbar if clear dialogue matters to you.

    “Great picture, but the built-in sound is weak and has no real bass.” R17, SampleMart

    “The built-in speakers sound thin though, and dialogue is hard to hear without a soundbar.” R2, DemoShop

  • The picture looks washed out and reflective when sunlight or lamps hit the screen.

    What reviewers did: One owner keeps the blinds half closed during the day and is happy with it (R28).

    How to avoid it: Avoid placing it opposite a window. Owners who control daylight are happier.

    “With sunlight coming through the windows the picture looks washed out and the glare is distracting.” R3, ShopLane

    “It is advertised as glare free, but in my bright room you still see reflections of lamps at night.” R24, DemoShop

  • A faint news or sports channel logo stays visible on grey screens.

    When it shows up: After about 8 months.

    What reviewers did: Running pixel refresh helped a little (R15).

    How to avoid it: Vary what’s on screen. Two heavy gamers report no burn-in after 5 to 7 months (R23, R34).

    “After eight months of daily use, mostly news and sports, I can see a faint retention of the channel logo on grey screens.” R33, SampleMart

    “After about eight months I can see a faint image retention of the news channel logo in the corner.” R15, SampleMart

  • One TV arrived with dead pixels; another shows faint banding in dark scenes.

    What reviewers did: One reviewer returned the TV for a refund (R7).

    How to avoid it: Check the screen on plain grey and black images while you can still return it.

    “Arrived with a cluster of dead pixels in the centre of the screen.” R7, ShopLane

    “I noticed faint banding in dark grey scenes, especially in fades to black.” R13, DemoShop

  • Cable broadcasts look soft and fast football stutters a little.

    How to avoid it: The same reviewer finds 4K streaming excellent, so low-quality cable is the weak spot (R9).

    “Excellent with 4K streaming, but cable broadcasts look soft and fast motion in football matches stutters a little.” R9, SampleMart
